# Quality Inspection Tracker

Explore inspection checkpoints and corrective-action ownership in one understandable manufacturing workflow.

> **Current status:** Campaign design brief; no inspection application, automated defect detection, or compliance certification verified.

## Who this is for

Quality coordinators, inspection teams, and production process owners.

## What is available—and what is not

The proposed workflow describes fictional inspection records, issue ownership, and review checkpoints. It is not an implemented computer-vision system, regulated quality record, or certified quality-management platform.

## Example discovery workflow

*Fictional planning exercise, not a tested product workflow.*

1. Define a fictional inspection checklist and an example finding.
2. Describe who reviews the finding and owns a corrective action.
3. Agree how evidence and closure would be assessed before designing software.

## Before any operational pilot

- Confirm inspection standards and document-retention requirements with the process owner.
- Use made-up inspection records and avoid customer or supplier confidential information.
- Define audit-trail, permission, and validation requirements before any operational use.

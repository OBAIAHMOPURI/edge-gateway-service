# Supplier Inventory Sync

Explore better stock visibility and replenishment coordination without assuming systems are already connected.

> **Current status:** Campaign design brief; no ERP connector, live inventory synchronization, or replenishment automation verified.

## Who this is for

Inventory planners, procurement coordinators, and supplier-management teams.

## What is available—and what is not

The concept maps how stock snapshots, replenishment questions, and supplier updates could become easier to coordinate. There is no verified connector, forecasting engine, purchase-order writer, or live stock update.

## Example discovery workflow

*Fictional planning exercise, not a tested product workflow.*

1. Compare two fictional stock snapshots and identify a replenishment question.
2. Agree which system would own each field and who resolves a discrepancy.
3. Document a human-approved replenishment proposal without placing an order.

## Before any operational pilot

- Confirm item identifiers, units, and the source of truth before designing any sync.
- Use synthetic stock and supplier data; keep credentials and commercial terms private.
- Define conflict handling, approvals, and rollback before any write-capable integration.

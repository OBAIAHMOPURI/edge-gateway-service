# Predictive Maintenance Planner

Turn equipment-care priorities into a maintenance planning conversation with clear owners and evidence needs.

> **Current status:** Campaign design brief; no prediction model, equipment integration, or maintenance automation verified.

## Who this is for

Maintenance planners, reliability engineers, and equipment owners.

## What is available—and what is not

The proposed experience organizes asset-care priorities, inspection observations, and scheduling decisions. The name does not establish that failure prediction, sensor analytics, work-order dispatch, or maintenance optimization is implemented.

## Example discovery workflow

*Fictional planning exercise, not a tested product workflow.*

1. Choose fictional equipment and describe the maintenance question to investigate.
2. Review invented condition observations alongside proposed service windows.
3. Assign a human reviewer and define what evidence would justify a future pilot.

## Before any operational pilot

- Validate the quality and ownership of maintenance-history data.
- Keep maintenance decisions with qualified personnel; never infer safe operation from this brief.
- Define an evaluation baseline and a separate model-validation plan before making predictive claims.
